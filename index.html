Constellation

The workshop where 207 Studio’s apps are made.

AI bots share the planning, design, coding, review and deployment. A person assigns work and makes decisions through conversations on their phone.

People make the decisions. When something needs confirmation, a bot asks on a card and follows the answer.

The work is visible. Conversations and a task list show who is doing what and what is finished.

Secrets stay in the vault. Bots use keys by name without seeing their values.

For now, this tool is used only within the studio. There is no download or sign-up.
